01 — About

Who I am

I'm Jimmy Li, a full-stack engineer who builds complete web applications, from the database to the interface. At Skrim I build an AI sales-training platform end to end: data models, APIs, LLM features, integrations and the UI.

On the side, I built CodeChat, an offline AI coding tutor powered by local language models. It runs entirely on your machine — no internet, no API costs, no data leaving your computer.

Before engineering, I worked in operations, management and sales: I ran a business to a 95% success rate, managed 200-person workflows, and hit sales targets through analysis rather than instinct. That same approach now goes into writing code.
